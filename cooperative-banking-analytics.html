The Industry Reality

Why Conventional Banking Reporting Fails Decision Makers

Cooperative banks operate in high-touch local economies, but management visibility is often trapped in spreadsheets and siloed branch reports.

The Challenges Faced by Small & Mid-Sized Banks

  • ✗
    Fragmented CBS & Spreadsheet Silos: Data sits across separate core banking exports, MIS spreadsheets, and trial balances with no single consolidated view.
  • ✗
    Delayed Decision Cycles: Branch managers and head office staff spend days manually formatting month-end numbers, meaning leadership reviews outdated indicators.
  • ✗
    Costly In-House Talent: Hiring and retaining specialized data engineers, BI architects, and quantitative analysts is prohibitive for 5 to 50 branch institutions.
  • ✗
    Blind Slippages into NPA: Overdue patterns, SMA transitions, and concentration risks often surface only when loans turn non-performing.

Strategic Methodology

From Raw Operational Records to Boardroom Clarity

We do not just visualize data. We guide leadership through a continuous 4-tier decision intelligence framework.

01

Descriptive

What happened?

Consolidates branch deposit shifts, fresh disbursements, CASA ratios, and transaction velocity across all locations.

02

Diagnostic

Why did it happen?

03

04

Prescriptive

8 Core Modules

Comprehensive Analytics Architecture for Cooperative Banks

Engineered to cover every critical dimension of small and mid-sized banking operations, risk, and profitability.

Module 01

Executive Overview Dashboard

High-level bird’s-eye telemetry for the MD, Chairman, and Board of Directors. Instantly view bank-level solvency, liquidity, and operational growth metrics.

Key Telemetry: Gross & Net Advances • Total Deposits • CASA Ratio • Fixed Deposit Maturity Spreads • Net Interest Margin (NIM) • Revenue vs Expenses & Operating Profit • Month-on-Month Trends.
Module 02

Branch Performance & Scorecards

Rank branches objectively with multi-factor scoring. Eliminate subjective bias and foster healthy operational accountability among branch managers.

Key Telemetry: Branch League Ranking • Deposit Growth vs Target • Loan Disbursement Velocity • Recovery & Overdue Performance • New Account Onboarding • Performance Scorecards.
Module 03

Loan Portfolio & NPA Early Warning

Granular risk classification and recovery monitoring. Track loans before they deteriorate into Non-Performing Assets to safeguard bank provisioning.

Key Telemetry: Outstanding Advances • Portfolio Sector Mix (Agri/Gold/MSME/Personal) • SMA-0/1/2 Aging Buckets • Overdue Recovery Trends • Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (LDR) • Provisioning impact.
Module 04

Deposit & Fixed Deposit (FD) Dynamics

Protect your deposit franchise. Track CASA movements, anticipate FD maturities, and detect high-value concentration risks across branch books.

Key Telemetry: Total Deposits • Current vs Savings vs Term Deposits • FD Maturity Ladders (30/60/90 days) • Renewal vs Outflow Ratio • High-Net-Worth Depositor Concentration.
Module 05

Customer & Member Intelligence

Understand customer behavior and member relationships. Track new account velocity, identify dormant accounts, and uncover cross-sell opportunities.

Key Telemetry: New Account Openings • Active vs Inactive/Dormant Ratios • Shareholding Member Analytics • Product Penetration Per Member • Branch Acquisition Efficiency.
Module 06

Transaction & Channel Telemetry

Monitor branch operational load versus digital channel migration. Assess cash counter pressure against digital transactions (UPI, ATM, NEFT/RTGS).

Key Telemetry: Transaction Count & Aggregate Value • Branch Counter vs ATM vs UPI • Peak Load Hours • Average Ticket Sizes • Digital Channel Adoption Rates.
Module 07

Financial Health & Profitability

Branch-wise P&L breakdown and cost containment. Clearly identify which branches are generating net interest margin surplus and which are operating at a loss.

Key Telemetry: Interest Income vs Interest Expended • Operating Overhead Costs • Branch Contribution Margin • Cost-to-Income Ratio • Spread Analysis.
Module 08

Management Intelligence & Action Items

Contextual synthesis and automated exception detection. Translates raw data patterns into 3 to 5 clear, concrete interventions for the next executive committee meeting.

Key Telemetry: Top & Bottom Performing Branches • 3-Month Trailing Trajectory • Critical Risk & Slippage Triggers • Prioritized Board Action Points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Addressing Board & Leadership Inquiries

Clear answers to common questions asked by Managing Directors, Chairmen, and General Managers.

“We already have MIS reports generated by our staff. Why do we need this?”

MIS reports tell you what happened historically in a static spreadsheet format. TechAstra transforms operational data into management intelligence: identifying why numbers changed, which branches are deviating, 3-month trailing slippage trends, and actionable interventions before board reviews.

“Our Core Banking Solution (CBS) already produces hundreds of reports.”

CBS systems are transactional databases designed for day-to-day cashier entries and ledger balances. They are not business intelligence platforms. We sit on top of your existing CBS to synthesize multi-branch comparisons, deposit concentration, and executive scorecards without disrupting daily counter operations.

“Can our bank share data without compromising security or regulatory compliance?”

Yes. You do not need to provide direct access to your internal database or confidential customer account names. We can ingest authorized, anonymized CBS report exports with strict NDA coverage, AES-256 encryption, and full audit logs adhering to cooperative banking standards.

“How long does it take to deploy and see the first monthly report?”

Because we utilize standardized CBS export schemas, onboarding typically takes only 7 to 14 days. Your bank receives its first complete Monthly Management Intelligence Review (MMIR) within the first full monthly reporting cycle.
