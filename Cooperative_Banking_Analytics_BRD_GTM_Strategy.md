Business Requirement Document + Go-To-Market Strategy

Working Concept: Analytics-as-a-Service for Small & Mid-Sized Cooperative Banks




# 1. Executive Summary

The proposed business will provide affordable, outsourced data analytics and business intelligence services to small and mid-sized cooperative banks. The service will convert existing CBS, MIS, financial, loan, deposit, FD, customer and transaction data into branch-level and bank-level insights.

The core proposition is simple: cooperative banks often have significant data but may not have the budget or specialist resources to maintain an internal analytics team. The company becomes an outsourced analytics and management-intelligence function, combining dashboards, monthly reporting, performance benchmarking and actionable insights.

# 2. Vision, Mission & Positioning

## Vision

Simplify complicated banking data into clear, actionable business decisions.

## Mission

Enable cooperative banks to become more data-driven without the cost and complexity of building an internal analytics function.

## Positioning

Cooperative Banking Analytics & Decision Intelligence

## Core Promise

Your data already tells the story. We make it visible, understandable and actionable.

# 3. Problem Statement

Data is distributed across CBS reports, Excel files, MIS reports and financial statements.

Management may not have a single consolidated view of branch performance.

Manual monthly reporting consumes staff time and can delay decision-making.

Smaller banks may find it difficult to hire and retain dedicated data analysts, BI developers and data engineers.

Branch-level trends, profitability, deposit movement, loan performance and risk indicators may not be visible in one place.

# 4. Proposed Solution

A secure Banking Intelligence Platform supported by an analytics service team.

Securely ingest authorized bank data from CBS exports, Excel/CSV files, databases or APIs.

Standardize and validate data.

Create bank-level and branch-level dashboards.

Track month-on-month and year-on-year performance.

Identify trends, exceptions and management attention areas.

Produce a recurring Monthly Management Intelligence Report.

# 11. Indicative Technology Architecture

Bank data → Secure ingestion → Data validation/ETL → Central data store/warehouse → Analytics/BI layer → Dashboards → Monthly reports & alerts

Initial ingestion: CSV/Excel/CBS exports

Later integrations: database connectors/APIs

Role-based access control

Encryption in transit and at rest

Audit logging

Data segregation by bank

Backups and retention controls

# 12. Data Security & Governance

Because the service may process sensitive financial information, security must be designed into the commercial and technical model from day one.

Least-privilege access

Multi-factor authentication

Encryption

Role-based dashboards

Audit trails

Secure data transfer

Clear data ownership and retention clauses

NDA and appropriate data-processing contractual terms

Documented incident response

Alignment with applicable RBI, cooperative-banking and contractual requirements

Important: Position Phase 1 as management analytics/decision support, not automated credit approval or regulatory decision-making.

# 13. Go-To-Market Strategy

## Market Entry Strategy

Start narrow: small and mid-sized cooperative banks in selected geographic clusters rather than attempting to target every bank nationally.

## 90-Day GTM Plan

Days 1–15: Finalize ICP, positioning, MVP scope, pricing framework, security framework, website and sales deck.

Days 15–30: Build a working prototype, demo bank dataset, sample dashboard and sample monthly management report.

Days 30–45: Build a 100-bank prospect list and identify MD/CEO/GM/CFO/operations contacts.

Days 45–60: Run discovery calls and demos; offer a controlled pilot/data-performance assessment.

Days 60–90: Convert 1–3 pilot banks into annual recurring contracts and create case studies.

# 15. Lead Magnet / Pilot Offer

## Free or Low-Cost Cooperative Bank Data & Branch Performance Assessment

Offer a limited assessment using a controlled and appropriately authorized dataset. Demonstrate how branch rankings, deposit/loan trends, recovery movement, transaction trends and financial indicators can be presented more clearly than conventional spreadsheets.

The objective is not to give away the full service; it is to demonstrate the management value of the platform.

# 16. Sales Pitch

## 30-Second Pitch

Most cooperative banks already generate enormous amounts of data. The problem is that the data sits across CBS reports, Excel sheets and MIS reports, so management does not always get one clear picture of what is happening across branches. We provide an outsourced analytics function that consolidates branch, loan, deposit, FD, transaction and financial data into management dashboards and monthly insights—giving the bank analytics capability without the cost of building a full internal team.

## MD / CEO Meeting Pitch

“Sir, I’m not here to sell you another software. Your bank already generates enormous amounts of data every day. Our job is to convert that data into management intelligence. We show you which branches are growing, which are declining, where deposits are moving, how loans and recovery are performing, and how your financial performance is changing month-on-month. Instead of hiring a complete analytics team internally, you get an outsourced analytics function from us at a predictable cost. Your data already tells the story. We help your management read it.”

# 17. Objection Handling

“We already have MIS.” We do not replace MIS. We convert existing reporting into management intelligence through trends, comparisons, branch benchmarking and actionable insights.

“Our CBS already provides reports.” That is useful for operational reporting. We bring relevant information together and focus on management-level questions across branches and business functions.

“We cannot share bank data.” We can start with a limited or appropriately anonymized dataset for the pilot and establish security, access and contractual controls before production use.

“We do not have budget.” The service is designed specifically to avoid the cost of building a complete internal analytics team.

# 20. Risks & Mitigation

Data security/privacy Security-first architecture, contractual controls, access restrictions and auditability.

CBS integration complexity Start with standardized CSV/Excel ingestion; build connectors after customer validation.

Long bank sales cycles Use a small pilot and a clear business case rather than selling a large implementation first.

Low willingness to pay Quantify management time saved and performance visibility; package as outsourced analytics capability.

Becoming a manpower-only service Productize dashboards, data models and recurring reporting; automate repetitive analysis.

Regulatory positioning Keep Phase 1 focused on management analytics and obtain specialist advice for regulated workflows.
