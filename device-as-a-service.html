The 10-Stage Managed Device Lifecycle

Every device in your fleet follows a rigorous, trackable lifecycle to protect data, maintain reliability, and eliminate downtime.

STAGE 01

Procure

Batch procurement of authorized Android hardware with standardized specifications, bulk volume pricing, and valid pan-India manufacturer warranty.

STAGE 02

Configure

Firmware initialization, OS hardening, pre-installation of client software, background daemon setup, and optional MDM kiosk lock provisioning.

STAGE 03

Tag & Register

Tamper-resistant barcode asset tagging, dual IMEI registration, serial indexing, and digital linking to specific partner contracts in the asset database.

STAGE 04

Quality Check (QC)

Multi-point hardware diagnostics: battery discharge benchmarks, camera focus calibration (for QR/visitor scan), display touch grid, and charging integrity.

STAGE 05

Deploy & Dispatch

Packaged with high-durability armor case, glass screen guard, and certified charger. Dispatched directly to the client site or society guard gate.

STAGE 06

Monitor & Track

Centralized visibility into fleet activation status, deployment location history, monthly billing validation, and preventive hardware health checks.

STAGE 07

Repair / Replace

24–48 hour rapid dispatch swap on malfunction. TechAstra interfaces directly with authorized OEM service centers, insulating you from warranty claims.

STAGE 08

Recover

Structured end-of-term recovery process when a society or client terminates software usage. Scheduled reverse pickup and physical inspection.

STAGE 09

Refurbish

Cryptographic data sanitization, physical deep cleaning, battery and casing replacement, and re-qualification for secondary fleet batches.

STAGE 10

Redeploy or Responsible Retirement

Certified healthy devices are immediately re-assigned to new software customers. Aged or sub-threshold units are safely liquidated or sent to certified e-waste recyclers.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions & Objection Handling

Everything your operations, finance, and software leadership need to know before deploying.

Why should we rent instead of buying the devices outright?

Buying devices ties up valuable capital in rapidly depreciating assets. More importantly, buying leaves you responsible for ongoing maintenance, battery replacements, broken screens, software updates, and asset tracking. Renting through TechAstra converts that burden into an affordable, predictable operating expense (OPEX) with guaranteed replacement SLAs.

What happens if a smartphone breaks or fails at a customer site?

TechAstra maintains an operational replacement buffer. When a hardware ticket is logged, we dispatch an identical, pre-configured replacement unit within our contractual 24 to 48-hour SLA window. The failed device is returned to us for diagnostic triage and OEM warranty repair.

What happens if a device is lost or stolen at a customer premises?

Our Master Service Agreement (MSA) specifies standard loss and damage policies. Devices can be locked remotely via MDM to render them unusable. A nominal replacement fee or optional device protection coverage applies, after which a fresh unit is dispatched immediately.

Can devices be locked to our application only (Kiosk Mode)?

Yes. We offer managed Kiosk Mode provisioning through Mobile Device Management (MDM). This prevents guards or field operators from browsing external websites, playing games, changing device passwords, or downloading unauthorized apps.

Can TechAstra scale to supply hundreds or thousands of devices?

Yes. The TechAstra DaaS model is built around structured monthly deployment forecasts and established OEM procurement partnerships. As your software customer acquisition expands, our batch staging pipeline scales directly to match your monthly rollout quotas.

How does billing and invoicing work?

You receive a unified, itemized monthly invoice based on the active device register. New devices deployed mid-month are prorated according to contract terms. No hidden maintenance charges or surprise repair invoices.
