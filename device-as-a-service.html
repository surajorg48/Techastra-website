Operational Excellence

The 10-Stage Managed Fleet Lifecycle

Every device follows a disciplined, trackable operational lifecycle to guarantee continuous uptime.

STAGE 01

Procure

Standardized Android hardware procured in batch volume with manufacturer warranty.

STAGE 02

Configure

OS provisioning, client application pre-installation, background daemon lock, and MDM setup.

STAGE 03

Tag & Register

Tamper-proof barcode asset tagging and dual IMEI indexing linked to the client's master contract.

STAGE 04

Quality Check (QC)

Hardware diagnostic battery run-down, camera barcode focus check, and charging port integrity.

STAGE 05

Deploy & Dispatch

Shipped with armored protective case, tempered screen guard, and charger directly to site.

STAGE 06

Monitor & Track

Centralized visibility into deployment status, monthly billing cycles, and location telemetry.

STAGE 07

Repair / Replace

24–48 hour rapid dispatch swap on malfunction; TechAstra handles service center claims.

STAGE 08

Recover

Reverse logistics collection and physical inspection when client contract terms conclude.

STAGE 09

Refurbish

Cryptographic data sanitization, physical deep cleaning, battery re-qualification, and testing.
