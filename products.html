Clinical & Healthcare Operations Active Production

TechAstra HMS

Cloud-Native Hospital Management & Patient Administration System

Problem: Hospitals and multi-specialty clinics grapple with fragmented record-keeping, misallocated hospital beds, delayed outpatient queues, and error-prone billing reconciliations across paper registers and disconnected software.
Solution: TechAstra HMS establishes a centralized digital command system connecting administration, consulting doctors, nursing wards, pharmacy, and patients with real-time state synchronization.

Core Operational Capabilities

OPD Queue Engine: Real-time patient token progression, doctor schedule availability, and consultation tracking.
Bed & Ward Matrix: Visual occupancy tracker categorizing ICU, General, and Private wards with instant intake/release triggers.
Multi-Role Portals: Distinct authenticated access dashboards for Hospital Administrators, Doctors, Receptionists, and Patients.
Itemized Billing: Automated cost calculation across clinical consultations, bed days, pharmacy prescriptions, and diagnostic lab tests.
Secure AWS S3 Storage: Medical histories, diagnostic lab PDFs, and patient identification documents safely archived with presigned URLs.
Doctor Scheduling: Duty rosters, slot reservations, appointment rescheduling, and department-wise allocation.

Target Healthcare Organizations

Multi-specialty hospitals, private nursing homes, outpatient clinics, diagnostic centers, and healthcare networks aiming to eliminate paper workflows and reduce patient waiting times.

Architecture & Technology Stack

Frontend: React (Vite) / Tailwind CSS Frontend Hosting: Vercel CDN Backend API: Node.js / Express Database: MongoDB on dedicated AWS EC2 Object Storage: AWS S3 (ap-south-1 Mumbai)

Real Estate Technology Active Production

Prop Hunt

Real Estate CRM, Wholesaling & Deal Intelligence Engine

Problem: Real estate wholesalers and brokerages operate with high transaction urgency. Relying on disorganized phone notes and basic spreadsheets causes expired inspection periods, miscalculated offer margins, and lost cash buyer deals.
Solution: Prop Hunt structures the entire deal lifecycle into an automated underwriting cockpit: instant ARV/MAO formulas, motivation-scored leads, 48-hour due diligence urgency alerts, and a cash-buyer disposition room.

Core Operational Capabilities

Dual Business Modes: Instant toggle between Wholesaling operations and licensed Real Estate Brokerage workflows.
Financial Calculators: Built-in After Repair Value (ARV), Maximum Allowable Offer (MAO), and Assignment Fee margin equations.
Interactive Kanban Pipeline: Drag-and-drop deal board with stage valuations, contract amounts, and velocity indicators.
Lead Motivation Scoring: Dual scoring combining automated heuristic rules with AI motivation analysis.
Due Diligence Urgency Alerts: Automated countdowns highlighting contracts entering their final 48 hours of inspection.
Enterprise Security: Two-Factor Authentication (TOTP QR codes) with admin-level security enforcement across team members.

Target Real Estate Professionals

Real estate wholesaling companies, property acquisitions managers, fix-and-flip investors, independent brokers, and property investment syndicates.

Architecture & Technology Stack

Framework: Laravel 12 (PHP 8.2) Database: MySQL (InnoDB) Styling: Tailwind CSS & Tabler Components Hosting: AWS EC2 (Ubuntu) with Elastic IP Domain & SSL: DuckDNS + Let's Encrypt Pipeline: Automated Cloud Deployment
