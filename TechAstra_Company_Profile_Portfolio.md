TechAstra is a technology and business-solutions company focused on simplifying complicated work through software, data, automation and managed technology services. # 7. Flagship Business Concepts

## TechAstra Banking Intelligence

An Analytics-as-a-Service offering for small and mid-sized cooperative banks. We consolidate authorized financial, branch, loan, deposit, FD, customer and transaction data into dashboards and recurring management intelligence.

Core proposition: Your data already tells the story. We make it visible.

Business model: recurring analytics service + implementation + optional advanced analytics.

## TechAstra Device Services

A Device-as-a-Service model supplying managed smartphones to B2B software companies and their customers. The initial use case is dedicated devices for society security and visitor-management deployments.

Core proposition: You acquire the software customer. We take care of the devices.

Business model: annual B2B contract + recurring per-device rental + optional managed services.
