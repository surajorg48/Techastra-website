Unlocking Cooperative Banking Intelligence

Urban Co-operative Banks (UCBs) and DCCBs sit on decades of transactional wealth locked inside legacy Core Banking Systems. BankIntelect liberates this data without risky system overhauls.

1. The Core Banking Blind Spot

Static Ledger Traps

Traditional CBS platforms record balances, not behavioral patterns. By the time an account crosses the 90-day threshold into NPA status, recovery options have already eroded.

2. The BankIntelect Advantage

Predictive Intelligence

BankIntelect models borrower inflow velocity, turnover trends, and SMA migration trajectories, alerting loan officers weeks before formal classification occurs.

3. Actionable Board Governance

Decisions with Clarity

Replace hundreds of fragmented spreadsheet exports with a unified, board-ready Monthly Management Information Report (MMIR) optimized for Director scrutiny and regulatory audits.

The Four Pillars of BankIntelect™

Engineered specifically for the regulatory and operational reality of Indian Cooperative Banks.

Pillar 01 • Credit Risk

3-Month Early Slippage Radar

Don't wait until an account crosses 90 days DPD to discover non-performing assets. BankIntelect's predictive heuristics track SMA-0, SMA-1, and SMA-2 migration trends, flagging borrower balance erosion before formal NPA classification.

Implementation Method

Zero CBS Modification. 100% Security.

BankIntelect operates as an intelligence layer on top of your existing CBS infrastructure without vendor lock-in or database modifications.

01

Non-Invasive Ingestion

Ingests standard anonymized daily/monthly end-of-day extracts or reports. Zero code changes required on your Core Banking System.

02

Heuristic Processing

Proprietary BankIntelect models calculate slippage likelihood, liquidity run-off probabilities, and cross-branch benchmarking in real-time.

03

Executive Boardroom Delivery

Visual dashboard outputs, interactive drilldowns, and automated Monthly Management Information Reports (MMIR) ready for immediate distribution.

Data Protection Guarantee

Bank-Grade AES-256 Vault Isolation

All bank data processed by BankIntelect is encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). Strict multi-tenant isolation, role-based access control (RBAC), and localized hosting ensure compliance with Indian banking privacy regulations.
